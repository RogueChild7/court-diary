Court Diary

A private court diary and case management app for an advocate's daily practice in Kenya.

What it does

Court Diary keeps track of court dates, mentions, hearings, rulings and filing deadlines. It runs on the advocate's own computer and stores its records there.

If the advocate chooses to connect a Google account, Court Diary creates its own "Court Diary" calendar in that account and adds each court date and deadline to it, so that Google Calendar can send email and phone reminders. It can also keep an encrypted backup in the app's private folder in Google Drive.

Privacy

Court Diary is used by a single advocate. It does not sell, share or analyse anyone's data. Read the privacy policy.
